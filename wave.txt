WAVE



Waves purpose is to show the relationship between Probability, Payout, and Stake.

These is only one concrete of Wave: Probability. Probability is the starting point. Probability converts itself into a multiplier.

Probability → Multiplier

The multiplier is calculated from probability.

Multiplier = 1 ÷ Probability

The multiplier is the bridge between probability and the money values.

From there, Wave has two operations.

PROBABILITY + STAKE → PAYOUT

If we know the probability and the amount of stake, Wave finds the payout.

Payout = Stake × Multiplier

Because the multiplier is derived from probability, this can also be written as:

Payout = Stake ÷ Probability

For example, if the probability is 60%, the multiplier is approximately 1.6667. A $100 stake produces a payout of approximately $166.67.

PROBABILITY + PAYOUT → STAKE

If we know the probability and the payout we want to produce, Wave finds the required stake.

Stake = Payout ÷ Multiplier

Because the multiplier is derived from probability, this can also be written as:

Stake = Payout × Probability

For example, if the probability is 60% and the desired payout is $166.67, the required stake is approximately $100.

The probability is constant within either operation. We are changing which money value we are solving for.

If we have the stake, Wave tells us the payout.

If we have the payout, Wave tells us the stake.

Wave gives us both directions of the same relationship.

Probability
↓
Multiplier
↓
Payout ↔ Stake

Wave can also display a probability range while keeping the payout fixed.

For example, if the fixed payout is $166.67, Wave can show the stake required at different probabilities.

100% → $166.67 stake
90% → $150.00 stake
80% → $133.34 stake
70% → $116.67 stake
60% → $100.00 stake
50% → $83.34 stake
40% → $66.67 stake
30% → $50.00 stake
20% → $33.33 stake
10% → $16.67 stake
1% → $1.67 stake

The payout stays fixed. Probability changes. Wave shows the stake required at each probability to produce that same payout.

Wave's one simple job here is to represent the economic relationship between Probability, Payout, and Stake.

The core machine is:

Probability
→ Multiplier
→ Payout or Stake

Probability + Stake → Payout

Probability + Payout → Stake

All money calculations use Decimal.

The purpose of Wave is to make the relationship visible across probability states. It lets us see what a fixed payout requires as probability changes and what payout a given stake produces at a given probability.
